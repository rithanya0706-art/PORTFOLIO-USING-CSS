SIH 2026 · Healthcare Concept

VisionGuard AI

VisionGuard AI is a student project concept focused on explainable AI for diabetic retinopathy screening, particularly in underserved communities.

The concept explores retinal image analysis using deep learning and Grad-CAM visualisations to highlight areas that may require closer review by a healthcare professional.

This is a student concept, not a clinically validated diagnostic system. Medical decisions require qualified healthcare professionals.
